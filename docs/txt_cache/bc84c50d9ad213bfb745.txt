for the Position of Existing Client Account Manager

Moscow
26 January 2025

Limited Liability Company “OFA” (ООО “ОФА”), a company engaged in the import and wholesale distribution of organic food products, hereinafter
referred to as the “Employer”, represented by General Director Kuznetsov Oleg Yurievich, acting on the basis of Articles of Organization, on the
one part, and CLIENT_055 Vyacheslavovna, a citizen of Russian Federation, born on 5 August 1980, identification/passport details:76 5304951
issued 20/08/2021 by MVD 77905, registered at:Kochnovskyproezd,4-1-41, Moscow, Russia, hereinafter referred to as the “Employee”, on the
other part, jointly referred to as the “Parties”, have entered into this Employment Agreement as follows.

1. SUBJECT OF THE AGREEMENT
1.1. The Employee is employed by the Employer in the position of Existing Client Account Manager.
1.2. The Employee’s commencement date shall be 26 January 2025.
1.3. This Employment Agreement shall enter into force upon its execution by both Parties and shall remain in effect for an indefinite period.
1.4. Employment under this Agreement shall constitute the Employee’s primary place of employment unless otherwise agreed by the Parties in
writing.
1.5. The Employee’s place of work shall be remote. Remote or hybrid work arrangements has been agreed by the Parties.
1.6. The Employee shall report directly to the Employer’s General Director.

2. JOB DUTIES, RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF THE PARTIES
2.1. The Employee shall diligently perform the duties of Existing Client Account Manager, comply with the Employer’s internal policies and
regulations, observe labor discipline and occupational health and safety requirements, maintain the confidentiality of the Employer’s trade secrets,
and adhere to professional standards and applicable law.
2.2. The Employee’s duties shall include, but not be limited to:
 managing and developing relationships with the Employer’s existing wholesale customers in the organic food products market;
 serving as the primary contact for assigned customers, responding to inquiries and ensuring timely resolution of commercial and operational issues;
 maintaining accurate customer profiles, contact details, contractual terms, order history and related records in the Employer’s systems;
 receiving and coordinating customer orders, forecasts and requests with sales, procurement, logistics, quality and finance teams;
 monitoring order fulfillment, delivery schedules, documentation, returns, complaints and payment status for assigned customers;
 identifying opportunities to increase repeat sales, expand product assortment and improve customer retention and satisfaction;
 preparing commercial proposals, price quotations, assortment presentations and other materials for existing customers within approved terms;
 coordinating customer-specific requirements concerning imported organic products, including product information, certification, labeling and supporting
documents;

 monitoring customer satisfaction, collecting feedback and promptly escalating material service, quality, supply or reputational risks to management;
 participating in customer meetings, assortment reviews, negotiations and other activities aimed at maintaining long-term commercial relationships;
 preparing regular reports on customer activity, sales, receivables, retention risks and development opportunities and promptly informing the General
Director of material deviations;

 maintaining the confidentiality of the Employer’s commercial, financial, supplier, customer, pricing and other confidential information.
2.3. The Employee shall have the right to be provided with work in accordance with this Agreement, to receive timely and full payment of wages, to rest
periods, to safe working conditions, and to all other rights provided for under the labor laws of the Russian Federation.
2.4. The Employer shall provide the Employee with work in accordance with this Agreement, provide the information and resources reasonably necessary
for the proper performance of the Employee’s duties, pay wages in the manner prescribed herein, and comply with the labor laws of the Russian Federation.
2.5. The Employer shall have the right to require the Employee to properly perform her duties, observe labor discipline, exercise due care with respect to the
Employer’s property and information, and comply with all lawful instructions issued by the Employer’s management.

3. REMUNERATION
3.1. For the performance of her employment duties, the Employee shall receive a monthly base salary of RUB 600,000.00 (Six Hundred Thousand Russian
Rubles and 00 Kopecks) before any taxes and mandatory deductions required by law.
3.2. The Employee’s salary shall be paid at least twice each month in accordance with the Employer’s internal policies and the requirements of the labor
laws of the Russian Federation.
3.3. Bonuses, incentive payments and other performance-based compensation may be granted by the Employer based on achievement of agreed
customer-retention, sales and service targets, the Employee’s individual contribution and the Employer’s financial performance.

3.4. Work performed on weekends and public holidays, overtime work, and the performance of the duties of a temporarily absent employee shall
be compensated in accordance with the labor laws of the Russian Federation and the Employer’s internal policies.
3.5. Deductions from the Employee’s salary may be made only in the cases and in the manner provided for by the laws of the Russian Federation.

4. WORKING HOURS AND REST PERIODS
4.1. The Employee shall work a five-day workweek with a total working time of 40 (forty) hours per week. Saturday and Sunday shall be the
Employee’s regular days off unless otherwise provided by the work schedule or a lawful written instruction issued by the Employer.
4.2. The starting and finishing times of the working day and meal and rest breaks shall be determined in accordance with the Employer’s Internal
Labor Regulations, the applicable work schedule or a separate lawful instruction issued by the Employer.
4.3. Taking into account the nature of the Employee’s position, the Employee may be required to perform urgent customer-related tasks and
participate in negotiations, customer meetings, trade events, presentations and business travel to the extent permitted by law.
4.4. The Employee shall be entitled to annual basic paid leave of 28 (twenty-eight) calendar days. The procedure for granting leave shall be
determined in accordance with the vacation schedule and the labor laws of the Russian Federation.
4.5. Upon the Employee’s written request, unpaid leave may be granted for family reasons or other valid grounds in accordance with applicable
law.

5. SOCIAL INSURANCE, GUARANTEES AND COMPENSATION
5.1. The Employee shall be covered by the mandatory social insurance system in accordance with the procedures and conditions established by
the laws of the Russian Federation.
5.2. The Employee shall be entitled to the guarantees and compensation provided for by the labor laws of the Russian Federation, the Employer’s
internal policies and this Employment Agreement.
5.3. Where the Employee is required to undertake business travel, the Employer shall reimburse the related expenses in accordance with
applicable law and the Employer’s internal policies.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
6.1. The Employee undertakes not to disclose any information that becomes known to her in connection with the performance of her duties,
including supplier and customer information, purchase prices, margins, product documentation, commercial terms, budgets, databases and other
confidential information of the Employer.
6.2. The confidentiality obligation shall remain in force throughout the term of this Agreement and after its termination to the extent provided by law
or a separate written agreement between the Parties.
6.3. Exclusive rights to works and other intellectual property created by the Employee in the performance of her employment duties shall be
determined in accordance with the laws of the Russian Federation. The Employee shall promptly provide the Employer with the relevant source
materials, files and information.

7. LIABILITY OF THE PARTIES
7.1. The Parties shall be liable for any failure to perform or improper performance of their obligations under this Employment Agreement in
accordance with the laws of the Russian Federation.
7.2. The Employee shall be subject to disciplinary and material liability in the cases and in the manner provided for by the labor laws of the
Russian Federation.
7.3. The Employer shall be liable to the Employee for any delay in payment of wages, any unlawful deprivation of the Employee’s right to work and
any other violation of the Employee’s rights in accordance with law.

8. TERMINATION OF THE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT
8.1. This Employment Agreement may be terminated on the grounds provided for by the labor laws of the Russian Federation.
8.2. In all cases, the effective date of termination shall be the Employee’s last working day, except where the Employee did not actually perform
work but retained her position in accordance with applicable law.
8.3. Upon termination, the Employee shall return to the Employer all documents, access credentials, reports, tangible assets, work materials and
other information obtained or created in the course of performing her duties.

9. FINAL PROVISIONS
9.1. Any amendments or supplements to this Employment Agreement shall be valid only if made in writing and signed by both Parties.
9.2. Any disputes arising between the Parties in connection with this Employment Agreement shall be resolved in accordance with the procedure
established by the applicable laws of the Russian Federation.
9.3. In all matters not expressly provided for in this Employment Agreement, the Parties shall be governed by the laws of the Russian Federation
regulating employment relations.
9.4. This Employment Agreement is executed in two counterparts of equal legal force, one retained by the Employer and the other delivered to the
Employee.
