~ 20 минут

Задание 6. Прочитай текст и выполни задания после него.
My Friend Ben
Hello! My name is Lucy. I want to tell you about my friend. His name is Ben. He is nine years
old. Ben is tall and he has got short brown hair and big blue eyes.
Ben loves animals. He has got a pet dog. Its name is Max. Max is black and white. Every
morning Ben walks with his dog in the park. Ben can ride a bike and play tennis, but he cannot
swim.
On Sundays, we like to meet and play computer games or watch cartoons together. Today is
Sunday, and Ben is visiting me. We are eating delicious chocolate cake right now!
